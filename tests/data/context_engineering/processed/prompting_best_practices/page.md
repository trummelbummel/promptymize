## Core Best Practices

### 1. Structure Your Prompts for Clarity

Put instructions first and context second. Use clear delimiters so the model can separate sections.

### 2. Be Specific and Descriptive

Specify length, format, tone, audience, and expected goal. Vague prompts produce vague outputs.

### 3. Use Positive Instructions

Say what to do instead of only what to avoid. Pair negative constraints with concrete alternatives.
